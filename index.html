Aether Tech: Wearable Interface for Agentic AI & the Smart Home

Mission: Make technology fit naturally into everyday life, so it feels intuitive and effortless and everyone can get more out of the tools they already have.

  • Founded Aether Tech, a hardware startup in USF CONNECT's Student Innovation Incubator. Our first product is a wearable ring that bridges agentic AI and smart home technology.
  • The goal is to remove the friction of pulling out a phone or opening an app. A gesture, tap, or glance triggers the action, which matters most in fast-paced, hands-busy environments and for people who have trouble moving around.
  • The ring combines an IMU for gesture recognition, capacitive touch for intentional input, a miniature camera for visual context, and haptic feedback, linked over BLE to a phone that runs the AI, OCR, and AI-agent commands.
  • Working through customer discovery, business model validation, MVP development, and pitch preparation with USF CONNECT mentors and an Executive-in-Residence.

MSEE Thesis · Aug 2026 – Present

STAR: Smart Telemetric Action Ring

  • Designing a ring-sized wearable gesture controller with an IMU, Bluetooth LE, haptic feedback, and Qi wireless charging for smart device control.
  • Developing a custom gesture classification approach on raw IMU time-series so users can define their own gestures from just a few training examples.
  • Built a from-scratch Python pipeline (sensor filtering, orientation tracking, gesture segmentation, matching) plus a physics-based ring IMU simulator for testing, and benchmarked several recognition methods against each other.
  • Designed it to reject everyday motion and ask for a retry when unsure, instead of firing the wrong command. Early results on simulated data reach roughly 95% top-1 accuracy with about 1% bad accepts; real-ring testing is next.
  • Visual: ten varied recordings of the same square gesture (noise, tremor, size, tilt) merged into one 3D probability "tube" used as the gesture's fingerprint.
